            02 / The Core Insight
            
Signal is the product. We start at verified_

            

              Connected is not installed. Installed is not verified. Most tools stop the moment OAuth succeeds. Coldstart does not mark your setup green until a deduplicated event lands in Meta.
            

          

        


        

          

            

              Stage 01 · Connected
              Where tools stop
            

            
OAuth or Token Saved

            
You logged into Facebook or pasted a Dataset ID. Zero events are flowing from your store yet, and legacy theme snippets may still be firing duplicates.

            
Status: 0% signal proof

          


          

            

              Stage 02 · Installed
              Still unverified
            

            
Snippet + Webhook Linked

            
Browser pixel code is in Shopify Customer Events and the orders/paid webhook is linked to Coldstart’s CAPI relay—but no checkout has tested it.

            
Status: Waiting on test event

          


          

            

              Stage 03 · VERIFIED ★
              Where Coldstart starts
            

            
Deduplicated & Proven

            
Browser fbq and Server CAPI send the exact same event_name + event_id (order_<id>), SHA-256 hashed PII is validated, and Meta returns events_received: 1.

            
Status: Ready to spend with confidence

            04 / Hard Honesty Contract
            
What is Live, Sandbox, and Sample today_

            

              Every screen in Coldstart labels its data source. Here is the exact capability matrix enforced by the codebase—no vapourware, no silent fallbacks.
            

          

          Checking /api/health…
        


        

          
          
          
          
          
        


        

          

            

              
Onboarding Website & Shopify Scan

              Live
            

            
Fetches real Shopify /products.json (up to 24 products with photos & prices), scrapes site markdown + brand palette/fonts via Firecrawl, and synthesizes 3 evidence-backed ICPs + Brand Book with Gemini.

            
Route: POST /api/plugins/onboard/actions/scan · Fallback: Labelled simulated preview if offline

          


          

            

              
Market Listening (Reddit, X, IG Hashtags)

              Live
            

            
Queries public conversations through treg routed search with Firecrawl web-search fallback, verifies post URLs, and tags buyer intent and themes via Gemini Flash-Lite (or labelled keyword heuristic).

            
Route: POST /api/plugins/research/actions/scan · Keys: TREG_TOKEN, FIRECRAWL_API_KEY, GEMINI_API_KEY

          


          

            

              
Competitor Meta Ad Library Teardowns

              Live
            

            
Searches Meta Ad Library (ads_archive via treg) by category or named rival across countries (IN, US, GB, etc.) and generates structured creative teardowns without inventing ROAS or spend.

            
Route: POST /api/plugins/research/actions/competitors · Needs: TREG_TOKEN

          


          

            

              
Studio Gemini Drafting & Workers AI Images

              Live
            

            
Drafts and rewrites posts for all 5 platforms using your Brand Book & ICP. Generates post visuals via Cloudflare Workers AI (FLUX schnell / SDXL Lightning / DreamShaper) stored in browser IndexedDB (coldstart-media).

            
Routes: /api/plugins/studio/actions/draft & /api/plugins/images/actions/generate

          


          

            

              
Meta CAPI Token Path & Deduplication Relay

              Live
            

            
Paste a Dataset ID + Conversions API access token to send real server events to graph.facebook.com today. Includes SHA-256 PII normalization, sealed cs_vault cookie, and public /api/collect/:siteKey relay.

            
Routes: POST /api/plugins/meta/token & POST /api/collect/:siteKey

          


          

            

              
Meta OAuth Login & Shopify Webhook Link

              Sandbox
            

            
Facebook Login for Business OAuth and Shopify store OAuth + orders/paid webhook registration run in realistic sandbox mode until META_APP_ID/SECRET, SHOPIFY_API_KEY/SECRET, and App Review are completed.

            
Routes: /api/plugins/meta/connect & /api/plugins/shopify/connect

          


          

            

              
Reddit & X One-Click Publishing

              Sandbox
            

            
OAuth connection and direct publishing for approved Reddit self-posts (with community rule confirmation) and X posts run in sandbox until REDDIT_CLIENT_ID and X_CLIENT_ID credentials are set.

            
Routes: /api/plugins/reddit/actions/submit & /api/plugins/x/actions/post

          


          

            

              
Approval Engine, Calendar, Ads & Fern & Form

              Sample / Browser-Local
            

            
The default Fern & Form workspace, approval state machine (each/batch/guarded-auto), calendar scheduling, and paid-ad campaign drafts live in browser localStorage. Zero money is ever spent.

            
Engine: js/workspace-state.js (14/14 unit tests)

          


          

            

              
Results & Attribution Charts

              Sample
            

            
Store session trends and traffic-mix bars in the Results tab are illustrative sample series with dithered bar edges. Attributed ROAS is explicitly shown as — (not connected), never faked.

            
Surface: app-v3.html#/results

          


          

            

              
Instagram Profile Reading in Onboarding

              Not Implemented
            

            
While you can enter your @instagram handle during onboarding for your brand profile, Coldie does not scrape or guess your Instagram profile posts. The scan explicitly reports Instagram as unavailable / not read.

            
Enforced in: lib/plugins/onboard.js

              09 / Honest FAQ
              
Questions founders ask before connecting_

            

          


          

            Q01
How do I send live Meta CAPI events today without waiting for Meta App Review?
+
            

              
In Workspace → Tracking → Use dataset ID + token, paste your Dataset (Pixel) ID and a Conversions API access token generated in Meta Events Manager. That path bypasses OAuth sandbox mode, validates directly against graph.facebook.com, and stores the token encrypted in the server’s cs_vault cookie.

            

          

          

            Q02
Does Coldstart really read my Instagram profile during onboarding?
+
            

              
No. Onboarding scans your live website (Shopify /products.json + Firecrawl copy, colours, and fonts). Instagram profile reading in onboarding is not implemented yet, and Coldstart explicitly marks it as unavailable / not read rather than inventing fake post counts.

            

          

          

            Q03
Can Coldie spend money on Meta ads or publish posts without my permission?
+
            

              
Never. There is no background cron job spending ad budget or auto-posting. Every draft requires explicit approval in Studio, editing a post resets its approval, and paid ad campaigns in the Ads tab are local review drafts only.

            

          

          

            Q04
Where is my workspace data stored?
+
            

              
Your onboarding progress (coldstart.onboarding.v1), handoff package (coldstart.handoff.v1), and workspace state (coldstart-workspace-v1) are stored locally in your browser’s localStorage, and generated images live in browser IndexedDB (coldstart-media). OAuth/CAPI tokens stay server-side in an AES-256-GCM sealed httpOnly cookie.
