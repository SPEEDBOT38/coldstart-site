coldstart / design system v0.1

Yellow,
cobalt,
dither_

Three ideas carry the brand: an acid yellow and cobalt pair, a wide slanted techno face for anything loud, and ordered dithering wherever a gradient, shadow or transition would normally go. Everything is defined in css/tokens.css.

01 / colour

Palette

Deep cobalt#242A8C · body on yellow

Night#12125E · dark sections

Ink#0D0F3A · outlines

Cream#FCF9ED · quiet sections

Cobalt light#5F6CE6 · dither on night

Periwinkle#A9C4FB · muted on night

Yellow shade#D6CF22 · dither on yellow

Pink#EA2A72 · focus, selection only

  • Sections take one of four themes with data-theme: yellow, blue, night, cream. Each sets background, display colour, body colour and button colours.
  • Yellow and cobalt always appear together. Pink is reserved for focus rings and text selection.
  • Outlines are 2.5px ink, matching the thick line on Coldie.

03 / dither

Dots, not gradients

density ramp · 8×8 bayer matrix · 4px cells

edge · data-dither="rise"
joins two section colours

field · "blobs vignette"
slow drifting shade

cursor · data-dither="cursor"
move the pointer over it

image · data-dither-img
1-bit, resolves on scroll

  • All four come from js/dither.js. Canvases are drawn at one pixel per cell and scaled up with image-rendering: pixelated; the dot colour is the canvas's CSS color.
  • Small parts use a CSS checker instead (var(--checker)): button shadows, the footer wordmark, the hover cursor.
  • With reduced motion on, fields are drawn once and images show undithered.
